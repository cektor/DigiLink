DigiLink, **lisanslı radyo amatörlerinin** internet üzerinden amatör telsiz ses bağlantısı (voice-link) ağındaki konferanslara, rölelere, linklere ve istasyonlara bağlanmasını sağlayan **bağımsız** bir istemcidir. Uygulamayı kullanmak için geçerli bir amatör telsiz lisansı ve ses bağlantısı ağında doğrulanmış bir çağrı işareti gerekir.

Bu politika şunlar için geçerlidir:

Uygulamayı yükleyerek veya kullanarak bu politikada açıklanan uygulamaları okuduğunuzu kabul etmiş olursunuz. Politikayı kabul etmiyorsanız lütfen uygulamayı kullanmayın.

- Mikrofon **yalnızca** PTT (bas-konuş) tuşuna bastığınız sürece (ekrandaki tuş, fiziksel tuş, kulaklık/Bluetooth tuşu, widget veya Hızlı Ayarlar kutucuğu) açılır.

- Ses **ALGSoft sunucularına gönderilmez**, ALGSoft tarafından **kaydedilmez ve saklanmaz**.

- İsteğe bağlı gürültü bastırma, otomatik seviye ve netlik EQ'su gibi ses işleme **tamamen cihazınızda** yapılır.

- Bağlıyken **Chat** sekmesinden yazdığınız metin mesajları, ses bağlantısı protokolü üzerinden **anlık olarak** bağlı olduğunuz konferans/istasyona ve oradaki diğer kullanıcılara iletilir.

### 5.3 Konum (Digi APRS) — isteğe bağlı

- Konum **yalnızca Digi APRS özelliğini siz açtığınızda** kullanılır. Özellik **varsayılan olarak kapalıdır.**
- Konum izni istenmeden önce uygulama, konumun ne için ve nereye gönderileceğini açıklayan bir bilgilendirme ekranı gösterir.
- Konumunuz, ayarladığınız aralıklarla (varsayılan 5 dakika) **yalnızca APRS-IS** amatör telsiz ağına (ör. `turkiye.aprs2.net`) çağrı işaretiniz, seçtiğiniz SSID, simge ve yorum metniyle birlikte gönderilir.
- **APRS-IS herkese açık bir ağdır.** Gönderilen konum ve çağrı işaretiniz `aprs.fi` gibi haritalarda **herkes tarafından görülebilir**, üçüncü taraflarca arşivlenebilir. Bu, ALGSoft'un kontrolü dışındadır.
- İsterseniz GPS yerine **sabit konum** (elle girdiğiniz enlem/boylam) kullanabilirsiniz; bu durumda cihaz konumu hiç okunmaz.
- Konum **ALGSoft sunucularına gönderilmez**, ALGSoft tarafından saklanmaz.
- Digi APRS açıkken ve uygulama arka plandayken konum, yalnızca **görünür bir bildirimle çalışan ön plan hizmeti** üzerinden kullanılır. Uygulama `ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION` iznini **istemez.**
- Digi APRS kapatıldığında konum kullanımı hemen durur.

Uygulama **kişiler, arama kayıtları, SMS, kamera, takvim, depolama alanının tamamı, yüklü uygulamalar listesi, erişilebilirlik hizmeti veya arka plan konumu** izinlerini **istemez.**

## 7. Arka Planda Çalışma ve Ön Plan Hizmeti

- "Arka planda çalış" seçeneği açıkken, bir istasyona bağlıyken uygulama ekran kapalıyken de bağlantıyı sürdürmek için **ön plan hizmeti** kullanır. Bu süre boyunca bildirim alanında **her zaman görünür bir bildirim** bulunur.
- Ön plan hizmeti türleri: `microphone` (PTT ile konuşma), `mediaPlayback` (gelen sesin çalınması), `location` (yalnızca Digi APRS açıkken).
- Bağlantıyı kestiğinizde veya "Arka planda çalış" seçeneğini kapattığınızda ön plan hizmeti durur.
- Ana ekran bileşeni (widget) ve Hızlı Ayarlar kutucukları yalnızca bağlantı durumunu gösterir ve bağlan/kes/PTT komutlarını iletir; ek veri toplamaz.

## 5. Data We Process and Why

### 5.1 Sign-in and station information

| Data | Purpose | Recipient |
|---|---|---|
| Callsign | Signing in, appearing in the station list, connecting | Voice-link network directory and relay servers, stations you connect to |
| Password | Sign-in (authentication) only | Only the voice-link network's sign-in servers |
| Name and location text (QTH) — **optional** | Shown as your station info on the network | Voice-link network and its other users |

As is inherent to amateur radio, when you connect, **your callsign, name, location text and online status are visible to other users of the network.** This is how the service works and is governed by the network's own rules.

### 5.2 Audio (microphone)

- The microphone is opened **only** while you press PTT (on-screen button, physical key, headset/Bluetooth button, widget or Quick Settings tile).
- Your voice is compressed with the GSM codec and relayed **in real time** to the conference/repeater/link/station you are connected to.
- Audio is **not sent to ALGSoft servers** and is **not recorded or stored** by ALGSoft.
- Anyone listening to the conference or station you are connected to can hear you; some stations may record transmissions under their own rules. This is outside ALGSoft's control.
- Optional audio processing such as noise suppression, automatic level and clarity EQ runs **entirely on your device**.

### 5.2.1 Text messages (Chat)

- Text messages you type in the **Chat** tab while connected are relayed **in real time** over the voice-link protocol to the conference/station you are connected to and its other users.
- Messages are **not sent to ALGSoft servers** and are **not stored** by ALGSoft. They are shown only in your device's memory during the session.
- Your messages can be seen by everyone in that conference; do not share sensitive information.

### 5.2.2 Blocking and reports

- **Block:** The list of stations you block is kept **only on your device** and is never sent anywhere. A blocked station's audio is not played, its chat messages are hidden and it no longer appears in your station lists.
- **Report:** When you report a station from within the app, the following is sent to an ALGSoft server (`digilink.algsoft.net.tr`): the reported callsign, the reason you choose, the details you type (optional), the conference/station you are connected to, your callsign, install ID, platform and app version.
- This information is used **only to review the report and enforce the [Terms of Use](https://digilink.algsoft.net.tr/terms.php)**; reports are reviewed within 24 hours, kept for **at most 1 year** and not shared with third parties.

### 5.3 Location (Digi APRS) — optional

- Location is used **only when you turn on Digi APRS.** The feature is **off by default.**
- Before the location permission is requested, the App shows a disclosure screen explaining what location is used for and where it is sent.
- At the interval you choose (default 5 minutes) your position is sent **only to the APRS-IS** amateur radio network (e.g. `turkiye.aprs2.net`) together with your callsign, chosen SSID, symbol and comment.
- **APRS-IS is a public network.** The position and callsign you send can be **seen by anyone** on maps such as `aprs.fi` and may be archived by third parties. This is outside ALGSoft's control.
- You may use a **fixed position** (latitude/longitude you enter) instead of GPS; in that case the device location is not read at all.
- Location is **not sent to ALGSoft servers** and is not stored by ALGSoft.
- While Digi APRS is on and the App is in the background, location is used only through a **foreground service with a visible notification.** The App does **not** request `ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION`.
- Turning Digi APRS off stops location use immediately.

### 5.4 Usage statistics

When the App starts and when your callsign changes, it sends the following to an ALGSoft server (`digilink.algsoft.net.tr`):

- A randomly generated **install ID** (a UUID; **not** a device, hardware or advertising identifier; lost when the App is uninstalled),
- Platform (`android`) and app version,
- Operating system version (e.g. "Android 14 (API 34)"),
- Device manufacturer and model,
- Device language,
- The callsign you entered in Settings.

The server resolves an **approximate country and city** from the request's IP address at that moment. For this lookup the IP address is passed to a geolocation service (ipapi.co). **Your raw IP address is never logged or stored.**

**Purpose:** to see which versions and platforms the App runs on, detect compatibility problems, improve the service and keep it secure.

### 5.5 Service status and update check

For service continuity and security, the App periodically reads the service status and update information for your version from an ALGSoft server. These requests carry only the **platform name, app version and your callsign.** Responses are small and contain no personal data.

### 5.6 Presence (DigiLink / Digi Voice badge)

- While connected to a network, every few minutes the App reports **your callsign, install ID, platform and app name** to the presence server shared by DigiLink and ALGSoft's Digi Voice app (`digivoice.algsoft.net.tr`).
- **Purpose:** to let other DigiLink and Digi Voice users recognize that you are connected through one of these apps when you talk (app badge).
- The App also asks whether the callsigns shown on your screen are connected via DigiLink/Digi Voice; **the full user list is never returned.**
- The record is deleted when you disconnect; if the connection drops it expires within **15 minutes** at the latest and is purged within **1 day** at the latest.

### 5.7 Network connection and IP address

- As with any Internet service, the servers you connect to (voice-link network directory/relay servers, APRS-IS servers, ALGSoft servers) **see your IP address** during the connection.
- Due to the voice-link protocol, stations you connect to directly and relay servers may also see your IP address.
- ALGSoft servers **do not store raw IP addresses.**
- The App reads network state (connected or not, network type) to keep the link alive across Wi-Fi ↔ mobile handovers; it does **not** collect your Wi-Fi network name (SSID), MAC address or nearby networks.

### 5.8 Live captions — optional

- Live captions are **off by default.**
- When enabled, incoming audio is converted to text **entirely on your device, offline,** with the open-source Vosk engine. **No audio or text is sent to the Internet.**
- The speech model for the language you choose is downloaded **once** from `alphacephei.com` on first use. This is a standard file download (that server sees your IP address); no personal data is sent.

### 5.9 Voice guidance and accessibility — optional

- Voice guidance for blind and low-vision users uses the **text-to-speech (TTS) engine** installed on your device. The text to be spoken (e.g. callsigns) is passed to your selected TTS engine and is not sent to ALGSoft. The data practices of your chosen TTS engine are governed by its provider's policy.
- When a screen reader (TalkBack) is on, the App makes accessibility announcements; it does **not** request an accessibility service permission.

### 5.10 Sharing (user-initiated)

The following are shared **only when you choose to,** through the Android share sheet, to the app you select:

- A received transmission as audio (WAV) or video (MP4),
- A net control report as text or CSV,
- The app log.

ALGSoft has no access to these shares; shared content is subject to the policy of the app you choose.

### 5.11 External links

Some links in the App (callsign validation page, `aprs.fi` map, developer websites, e-mail) open in your browser or e-mail app. Those sites are governed by their own privacy policies.

## 13. Data Security

- All communication with ALGSoft servers is encrypted with **HTTPS (TLS).**
- On-device data is kept in Android's protected, app-private storage that other apps cannot access. On Android 10 and later this storage is encrypted by the operating system.
- Your password is never sent to ALGSoft, never written to logs and never included in UI-state saves.
- App logs are sanitized to exclude passwords, IP addresses and coordinates.
- Server data is accessible only to authorized administrators.
- **Important:** The voice-link network protocol and APRS-IS are open amateur radio protocols that historically operate **unencrypted.** In addition, amateur radio regulations in many countries prohibit encrypting the content of amateur transmissions. Audio and position data sent over these networks are therefore **not encrypted** and **should not be considered private.** Do not share sensitive information.
- No system is 100% secure; in the event of a data breach we will notify the relevant authorities and affected users as required by applicable law.
